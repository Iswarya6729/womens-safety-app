# Women's Safety App

## Overview

Women's Safety App is a community-driven project designed to enhance personal safety through emergency assistance features. The application enables users to quickly send alerts, share their live location, and contact trusted individuals during emergencies.

## Community Project

This project was developed as part of a social impact initiative to address women's safety concerns and provide quick access to emergency support.

## Problem Statement

Many women face situations where immediate access to help is critical. Traditional emergency response methods may take time, making it important to provide a fast and reliable safety solution.

## Objectives

* Provide instant emergency assistance.
* Enable quick communication with trusted contacts.
* Improve personal safety awareness.
* Support rapid response during emergencies.

## Features

* SOS Emergency Alert
* Live Location Sharing
* Emergency Contact Management
* One-Tap Emergency Calling
* Safety Tips and Guidelines
* Nearby Police Station Information
* User Registration and Login
