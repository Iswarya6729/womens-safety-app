What We Offer

Built for every situation
you might face

From late-night commutes to everyday situations — SafeHer has a feature for every moment.

🚨

One-Tap SOS Alert

Instantly send your GPS location, a distress message, and an audio clip to your trusted contacts and local authorities. Works even without internet.

📍

Live Location Sharing

Let chosen contacts track your journey in real-time until you mark yourself safe.

🎙️

Discreet Recording

Silently record audio and video in the background, automatically backed up to cloud.

🛡️

Safe Route Planner

Add up to 10 emergency contacts who receive real-time alerts and can see your location.

Simple & Fast

How SafeHer works

01

Set up your circle

Add trusted contacts — family, friends, or neighbors — who will be alerted in an emergency.

02

Enable safe walk

Before heading out, activate Safe Walk mode. Your contacts can follow your route live.

03

Press SOS if needed

One tap triggers alerts to all contacts, shares your location, and starts recording.

04

Get connected to help

Real-time notifications connect you to nearby volunteers, helplines, and support.
