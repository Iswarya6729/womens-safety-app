🛡️ Personal Safety Tips

🌙

Night Travel Safety

  • •Share live location before heading out
  • •Use well-lit, populated routes
  • •Prefer verified cabs over auto
  • •Charge phone before leaving
  • •Keep SafeHer SOS ready
🏠

Home Safety

  • •Install door/window alarms
  • •Know your neighbors
  • •Use video doorbells
  • •Keep emergency numbers visible
  • •Have a safe word with family
📱

Digital Safety

  • •Use strong, unique passwords
  • •Enable 2-factor auth
  • •Review app permissions
  • •Don't share location publicly
  • •Block/report online harassment

⚖️ Know Your Rights

🛡️ Zero FIR Right

You can file an FIR at ANY police station regardless of jurisdiction. The police cannot refuse to file it.

🌙 Sunset Law

You cannot be arrested after sunset and before sunrise unless a magistrate's order has been obtained (for women).

👩‍⚖️ Women Police Officer

You have the right to be examined by a female police officer only. This is non-negotiable.

📞 Free Legal Aid

You are entitled to free legal representation under the Legal Services Authorities Act. Call 15100.
