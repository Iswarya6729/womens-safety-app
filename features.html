Core Feature

🚨 One-Tap SOS Alert

When you're in danger, every second counts. A single tap on the SOS button instantly sends your GPS coordinates, a pre-written distress message, and a 30-second audio recording to your entire trusted circle — even without internet, using SMS.

  • ✓Works without internet via SMS fallback
  • ✓Sends real-time GPS location link
  • ✓Auto-uploads audio evidence to cloud
  • ✓Shake-to-activate for hands-free use
  • ✓Custom alert messages per contact

Navigation Safety

🗺️ Safe Walk Mode

Activate Safe Walk before any journey. Your trusted contacts get a live map view of your route in real-time. If you deviate from your path or don't arrive in time, they're automatically notified.

  • ✓Live GPS tracking visible to contacts
  • ✓Route deviation alerts
  • ✓Estimated arrival countdown
  • ✓Auto-SOS if you stop unexpectedly
Community Power

🌐 Safety Heatmap

A living, breathing safety map powered by the community. Women mark unsafe areas, safe zones, and helpful locations. The more people contribute, the safer the map becomes for everyone.

  • ✓Crowd-sourced unsafe area alerts
  • ✓Safe zone & shelter directory
  • ✓Incident reports with timestamps
  • ✓Anonymous reporting option
